Before you rely on any of these

None of them knows whether something is true. They check whether a claim can be checked, whether permission was granted, and whether a record was altered. A sentence can pass every check here and still be wrong — that is not a fault, it is the design. Use them to find your questions, then take those questions to a person.

Everything on this page is a file on your own machine. Open any of them straight from the folder — no server needed. The gate itself is a program you run: bash weir/install.sh.
