What this gate cannot do It stops what comes through it. It cannot stop a program that does not come through it at all — a program that opens its own connection is unaffected. Making it unavoidable is a job for the machine underneath: a network with no other way out, or a sandbox. That part is real and standard, and it is not built here. A gate you can walk around is a gate you should not rely on alone.
