Novora

Nine tools for pressing what you are told.

Paste something in, and get back what it is actually made of — before you believe it, sign it, act on it, or accept it. Everything runs on your own device. No account, no internet connection, nothing uploaded.

Read this before you trust a number here These are screens, not judges. They look for the words and structures that usually accompany a problem — they do not understand what you pasted, and they cannot tell whether it is true, legal, or safe. A high score means "nothing obvious was caught", never "this is fine". When the answer matters, use these to find your questions, and take those questions to a person who is qualified to answer them.
