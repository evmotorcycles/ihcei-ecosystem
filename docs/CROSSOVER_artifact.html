What “governance, not theology” can honestly mean

The five questions are governance questions. But the defensible version of that claim is narrower than it looks — and stating the narrow version is what makes it testable:

Each of the five admits an operational reading that names a measurable quantity. That is what makes a question governance-tractable. It is a Layer 2 claim about tractability — not about what the questions ultimately mean, and not evidence that any particular reading is correct.

The crossover protocol has four steps, and step 4 is not optional — a crossover that skips it is renaming, not reframing. Every reframe earns exactly one verdict: rival theory, interpretation, or category error.

Arm 1 · T2O

Quantum physics — Two-Hop Objectivity

Step 1 — the RT reading at full strength

Decoherence theory: a system coupled to an environment loses coherence in the pointer basis, and measurement statistics are recovered with no conscious observer. Settled, not in dispute.

Quantum Darwinism adds the part that matters. The environment is not a monolithic sink — it stores redundant copies, and observers access only fragments. It already separates total system–environment correlation from what a fragment-limited observer can obtain. Any reframe that “discovers” this has rediscovered an existing theory, and has to say so.

Step 2 — the channel

U = pointer entropy · D_enc = 1 − |Γ|, set by the coupling · D_dec = m/N, set by the observer. Two different knobs — which is what makes a two-hop claim testable rather than a relabelling.

The second hop is not dead weight — it makes the prediction 195× worse

The locked spec recorded Q3 as at genuine risk and gave the reason in writing: “the partial-information plateau means a form linear in fragment fraction must undershoot… I expect the two-hop linear form to LOSE this gate.” It did.

Q2 passed, and that pass is hollow. The linear form beat the quadratic by 0.22 bits, so the gate is met. Both are useless next to the single-hop form. A gate can be met by beating a worse rival, and that is what happened.

My prediction about where it would fail was wrong

I said the linear form would fail in the saturation region, at large fragments. It fails worst at small fragments — mean error 0.6787 at the smallest fraction against 0.3434 at the largest. Redundancy means one qubit in twenty already carries nearly all the classical information, so the exact curve is at the plateau while the linear form still predicts a twentieth of it. Direction right, location wrong. Recorded, not repaired.

The two-hop product form assumes the decode hop is scarce. Where records are redundantly copied, it is not. E = U · D_enc · D_dec now carries a stated domain limit: do not apply it to channels whose records are redundantly replicated. This does not reverse yeast, GitHub or the financial cohort — it marks a boundary, discovered by carrying the form into a new field and having it fail.

Verdict INTERPRETATION — not rival theory

Declared before the run. The channel identification is quantum Darwinism; it changes no prediction of physics. Declaring it in advance is what stopped a good numerical fit being presented afterwards as a physical discovery. There is something modest to take: a reframe that lands on an existing, well-tested theory is a validity check on the method — the vocabulary mapped onto real structure instead of inventing new structure.

Hard constraint respected: decoherence is not observation. Nothing here makes any outcome depend on a mind, and no signalling claim appears anywhere.

The experiment a physics lab would run

Prepare a system qubit dephased by an engineered N-subsystem environment — trapped ions, photonic multi-mode, or an NV centre with a controlled nuclear-spin bath. Hold the total decoherence factor |Γ| fixed while redistributing the per-subsystem overlaps: concentrate the record in a few strong couplings, versus spreading it thinly across many. Reconstruct I(S:F) against fragment size; extract redundancy Rδ.

It settles whether accessible information at fixed total decoherence depends on how the record is distributed. It settles nothing about governance, institutions or the five questions. And it is close to experiments already performed on quantum Darwinism in photonic and NV systems — offered as the experiment this arm implies, not an unprecedented one.

Arm 2 · T2R

Cognitive psychology — Two-Hop Retention

Step 1 — the RT reading at full strength

Encoding, storage, retrieval. The testing and spacing effects are robust and replicated. Bjork's New Theory of Disuse already posits two factors — storage strength and retrieval strength. A two-factor reading of memory is not new and is not claimed as new.

What is actually at issue is narrower: do the two combine multiplicatively, so one at zero cannot be compensated, or additively, so they trade off?

Step 4 — the discriminating prediction

The multiplicative form forbids what the additive form permits: with one factor at or near zero, retention must go to zero regardless of the other. An additive account predicts a non-zero floor. That corner of the design space is a real experiment.

The arm could not fail — and that is my error

Recovery was 1.0000 in all 36 configurations across all 200 replicates. The locked design applies noise per participant and then averages over the cell, so effective noise on a cell mean is sd ÷ √n — about 0.0045 at the lowest setting. The two accounts differ by far more than that everywhere.

C2 failed for want of a populated failing region. C3 “passed” at 1.0000 and C4 “failed” at a gap of exactly zero. Neither is informative. A test that cannot fail is not evidence. The noise levels were specified without accounting for averaging — mine. The spec is not edited and the gates are not re-scored.

One sentence of my own pre-registration is withdrawn

The spec said a C5 miss would be “a real result against DCM.” It is not, and that sentence is withdrawn. With recovery identically 1.0 there is no variation for Δ to predict, so the AUC is undefined, not low. C5 scores as not met because the locked rule says so — but its status is UNTESTABLE-HERE, not REFUTED. Nothing in this run is evidence for or against DCM.

What the next spec must do: put the noise on the cell mean directly, or raise it until averaged noise is comparable to the gap between the accounts — and re-register before running.

Verdict INTERPRETATION

Assigned by the gate rule declared in advance: the accounts separated everywhere in the tested design space, so the reframe added no design guidance. And it rests on a mis-specified noise model, so even the interpretation verdict is weakly supported here.
