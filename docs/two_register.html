The nameNamed from the mechanism, not from a tradition

The morphological sweep was used only as a design generator. Invariant: a claim under stress. Sweeping the operators gave holder / claim / the place a claim is sorted to / the cascade / what forces the sort. The locus cell was empty in every previous design, and filling it is the whole model.

Recovery register 16.1 claimant value shortfall

Fixed claims. The residual survives a shock, so later inflows still reach the holder.

Containment register 121 secondary failures

Participation claims. Loss is absorbed with no default event, so it does not propagate — but the claim extinguishes.

Neither instrument wins both: fixed claims cost 148 secondary failures, participation costs 94.4 in shortfall. A test asserts the model name and the entire results file contain no Arabic or theological terms — the design must be judged on its mechanics.

What diedRouting is beaten by chance

The claim under test was never "does mixing help" — two instruments with different failure modes usually beat either alone, and the spec writes that gate off as weak in advance. It was that assigning claims to registers by a contagion-risk signal beats assigning the same share at random.

The routing signal is mildly anti-predictive. It is not a size proxy — it is simply wrong. Routing on it puts participation on the wrong nodes, so a coin flip does better.

This is the fifth falsified selection rule in this programme, and the pre-registration said so before the run: "EXPECTED TO FAIL… four selection rules have been falsified, one of them with an INVERTED sign." The model therefore ships without the routing claim, as a fixed policy mix needing no scoring apparatus — cheaper and more robust than what was proposed.

Disclosed, not repaired

The combined objective was locked with equal 0.5/0.5 weights. On this substrate the shortfall term spans 18.17 and the cascade term spans 0.20 — shortfall dominates 90×, so J is effectively a shortfall metric despite its weights.

Nothing was re-scored and no threshold moved. The consequences: N1, N3 and N5 largely restate "shortfall favours fixed claims" and their interpretation is compromised. N2 and N4 are untouched — same-share comparisons and a diagnostic AUC.

Under a post-hoc range-balanced version of the same two objectives, an interior optimum does appear at share 0.60 (J′ 0.3167 against 0.5000 at both endpoints). So "the two-register idea is refuted" would be too strong. What is refuted is routing.

Distributing inflows continuously is the single largest effect in the entire financial programme — and it appeared in no proposal as a named component. It is not risk-sharing, not full reserve, not pooling. It is the discipline of paying holders down as money arrives instead of warehousing it.

AdoptionThe same pitch to both sectors

Conventional banks
  • Continuous distribution on distressed exposures — the largest measured effect by two orders of magnitude.
  • Full reserve beats leverage. Measured: m=1 → 0 failures, m=3 → 3,262, m=5 → 3,912, m=10 → 4,362.
  • Participation as a circuit breaker, not a return strategy — 18–32% fewer knock-on failures.
  • Do not buy a risk-routing model. Ours lost to 18 of 20 coin flips.
Islamic banks
  • Full reserve is vindicated on measurement, not doctrine — ΔU = 0 was the smoothest configuration tested. No theological premise required.
  • But risk-sharing does not lower your losses. Measured, it raises them — 94.4 against 16.1 — because writing a claim down extinguishes it and forecloses recovery.
  • Its real value is systemic. Sold as prudential infrastructure it is defensible; sold as a superior return profile it is not.
  • The audit story is the differentiator. The oxymoron charge lands because labels are unverifiable; a hash-chained register makes the label testable.

A sector that adopts participation expecting lower losses will be disappointed by arithmetic, not by markets.

The pointWhy 0/5 is the deliverable

The strength on display is not the model. It is the method engaging the telemetry: the model was designed from prior measurements, its central new claim was pre-registered with its own predicted failure written down, the failure arrived on schedule, and the model shipped smaller.
