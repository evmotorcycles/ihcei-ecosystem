The correction that matters most · C6The political finding was circular — and it reverses

D_dec was a raw count of hearings. Raw counts grow with domain size:

spearman(capacity U, raw hearing count) = +1.00 ← perfect rank correlation

So E = U·D was approximately U², and "Govt Operations has the highest realized yield" reduces to "Govt Operations is the biggest domain."

Rebuilding D_dec as hearings per enacted law — an intensity, not a size — breaks the circularity (ρ = −0.80) and reverses the conclusion:
