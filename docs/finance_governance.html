MethodThree harness corrections, all disclosed

None converted a mechanism failure into a pass — the mesh lost C5 before and after all three. Each was forced by a degenerate measurement, not a disliked answer.

  • The freeze was first sized against the initial base; after the replay drains the system that removed 100% of everything left, and every level returned an identical, meaningless 1.000.
  • The strike first landed after the whole replay, when the centralised book had cleared to 0.0. An empty book cannot be stressed.
  • Issuance ran once at seeding, so the centre still carried nothing at strike time.

Corrections to the narrative as received

The Rothschild–Waterloo story is substantially legend. The claim that advance news of Waterloo produced a market coup traces largely to Satan, an 1846 pamphlet by Georges Dairnvaell that was explicitly antisemitic propaganda; later archival work finds no such coup. The structural point survives without it — a cross-border sovereign debt market genuinely was built in this period. The anecdote should be dropped, not softened.

"Pay off the debt and the money supply collapses overnight" is overstated. Bank deposits are extinguished as loans are repaid, so most broad money contracts — central bank reserves and physical currency do not vanish. The defensible version, that broad money is predominantly a by-product of lending, is enough for the argument.

None of this section is evidence about how a payment system behaves. It is context. The evidence is the engine output above.
