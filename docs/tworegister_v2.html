Worked exampleSam's print shop gets his loan

Illustration, not measurement

Numbers invented to show the mechanism. The evidence is the sweep above, from 10,000 committed real events. The two are kept strictly apart.

Sam needs £20,000 for an industrial printer. The shop takes about £8,000/month.

1 · the money exists

The £20,000 comes from pooled savings that already exist. Nothing is created at a keyboard.

2 · the bank buys the printer with him

Not a loan at interest — a joint purchase. Sam puts in £6,000 (30%), the bank £14,000 (70%), and the bank holds real title to its share.

3 · he pays as he collects

Monday he invoices £3,000. Wednesday the customer pays — and that hour £1,500 goes to the bank, buying out a slice of its ownership. £1,500 stays with Sam. No monthly due date exists.

4 · the bad quarter

Biggest client leaves; revenue drops to £2,000. He passes through £1,000. That is the whole obligation. He is not in default. He was never late.

5 · the flood

£6,000 of stock destroyed. The bank owns 70% of the asset, so it carries its 70% of the loss — that is what holding title means. Sam keeps trading and keeps paying his supplier.

6 · when participation gets used

Sam's shop is not a systemic node — so it doesn't. If he were the sole packaging supplier to eleven other businesses, then a participation tranche earns its cost: you would be buying cascade protection and paying ~4.6× in recovery for it, knowingly.

ExtensionHow LISM grows from this — and from Al-Qudah

LISM was E = U · D_enc · D_dec — a two-hop channel coupling linearly. Two extensions came out of this programme, both forced by measurement:

  • Ddec has a timing dimension, and it dominates. LISM treated distribution fidelity as how well value propagates. The measurement says when it propagates is worth ~50× more than any structural choice about the channel. Distribution is not a coefficient — it is a rate.
  • The two hops have different failure modes and are not interchangeable. Recovery protects the holder; containment protects the network. What's new is that optimising one actively costs the other — 16% cascade for 4.6× shortfall — so the product can't be maximised by improving both independently.
And a third extension, from Al-Qudah — the one that surprised us

His amortising co-ownership makes D_dec structural rather than discretionary: value returns continuously because the contract is built that way, not because a policy says so. That is a stronger implementation of the same insight than ours — and the measurement says so, at 36.1 against our 71.0.

Echo is the load-bearing stack component here: append-only, hash-chained, Merkle-provable, it is what makes "which register was this claim in, and was it altered?" checkable. The oxymoron charge lands because labels are unverifiable; that is the answer to it. HELM runs the kernel on-device with no network call, so a participant is audited without their book leaving the premises.
