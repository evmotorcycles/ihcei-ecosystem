05
One substrate of five · our own manuscript refuted

A first-principles graph-topological scarcity metric

The gap as posed cannot be closed. A topological measure needs a graph: yeast and GitHub commit none, the quantum arm is a derivation with no dataset, and PyPI is circular — its declared outcome is derived from the same graph the metric would run on. That leaves interbank.

The metric has no free parameter, and LISM’s own §3.3c domain limit failed against it — in the opposite direction to its claim.

The finding nobody was looking for

Gap 1’s post-hoc diagnostic — explicitly not pre-registered, scored nothing, and by some distance the most important thing in this batch.

15 of the 20 artifacts return the identical verdict 0.1428 — under both engines. That concentration is why Δ failed. So the question became: what are the engines responding to?

Engine Spearman vs declared manipulativeness Spearman vs word count
HELM v1 — shipping+0.0445 −0.4831
HELM v2 — density-weighted+0.0388 −0.4774
Both engines score longer text as less manipulative, to nearly the same degree — and neither orders the manipulativeness gradient at all. Mean verdict by band is flat and mis-ordered: the “strongly manipulative” band scores below “mildly pressuring”.

It is not v2’s density weighting. v1 shows −0.4831 and v2 −0.4774 — the same number. The length effect lives in the gate and regex structure both engines inherit. Replacing v2 would change nothing.

What it costs us. On the DES set and the HELM v2 held-out set, the manipulative texts were also the short texts. This spec broke that confound deliberately — a 2-word “Act now.” in the manipulative band, three 30-word texts in the factual band — and the signal vanished. That is consistent with G = 0.2980 having measured length all along.

The limit, stated first. The band labels are mine, not independent raters’. So HELM is not refuted and W8 stays untestable. What carries weight is the direction: author labels are biased toward agreeing with the author’s engine, and the correlation still came out at zero.

Q4’s status therefore reverts to not licensed, and is in a worse position than before this run. The S half of shield-and-signal is unaffected — it’s a within-artifact deviation and doesn’t depend on the gradient — so the “rock” finding stands. The correction is recorded on HELM_V2.md rather than quietly edited out.

And none of it transfers. A cellular automaton is not an institution. Nothing here is evidence that any organisation is computationally irreducible, or that τ_v is the right thing to watch.

Gap 5 — the manuscript amended, not defended

Scarcity as local bridge fraction: the share of a node’s neighbours with which it shares no common neighbour. An edge whose endpoints share no neighbour has no alternative path of length two. No threshold chosen, no weight fitted, no free parameter.

Stratum U·D_enc·D_dec U·(D_enc+D_dec)² Advantage
High scarcity · B = 1 · n = 131 0.4455 0.4383 +0.0072
Low scarcity · B < 1 · n = 1,218 0.61590.6180 −0.0021
In the scarce-decode stratum both forms are anti-predictive. Where the decode hop has no two-step substitute is where the model performs worst — the opposite of what §3.3c asserts. Difference-in-advantage +0.0093 against a pre-registered +0.05; 90% CI [−0.0283, +0.0424], width 0.0707 against a 0.20 precision bar. The interval excludes the predicted effect.

What was withdrawn, and what wasn’t. The quantum result stands — the two-hop product really is 195× worse there. What is withdrawn is the explanation: that decode-hop scarcity separates the domains where the product form applies from those where it doesn’t. On the one substrate where scarcity is measurable independently of the outcome, it separates nothing.

LISM_manuscript_REVISED.md now carries §3.3c(i) with the amendment, citing the spec hash. The spec committed to that before the run, and a test asserts the manuscript actually changed — so it can’t stay a promise.

What the floor did

Δ = V·I·C has now voided five consecutive runs. It was not moved, and this batch found out why it keeps biting.

Every one of them landed on a small lattice. On the two continuous outcomes in this batch — AUC and an AUC difference — Δ cannot fail: V and C sit near 1 by construction. So it was recorded excluded in both, rather than counted as a pass that would inflate the score.

That is a scope limit on our own instrument, found rather than assumed: DCM is an admissibility check for concentrated or categorical outcomes, and it is silent everywhere else.

Still not known

Whether HELM detects manipulation at all. Closing it needs manipulativeness labels from raters who did not build the engine, on texts where length and manipulativeness are decorrelated by design. Until then G is a number whose referent is unknown.

Whether any institution is computationally irreducible. Nothing in the repository demonstrates it, so the Q5 testbed licenses claims about cellular automata and nothing else.

Whether the scope rule holds across substrates. Three of five commit no graph and a fourth is circular. One substrate cannot establish a cross-substrate rule, and reporting it as though it could would be the error the spec was written to prevent.

Whether d² = κ·τ_rt holds. Blocked on hardware. Four named lab experiments, none run.
