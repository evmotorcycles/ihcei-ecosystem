# OQM: the method, not the conclusions

This skill covers **how a governance reading is derived**. The readings themselves live in
`oqm-governance-vs-theology`; the empirical status of any claim lives there too. Keep the
two separate — a correctly executed method does not make a conclusion true, and this skill
exists partly to make that gap visible.

**Everything this skill produces is `[L2]`.** A method for reading a text yields readings.
No amount of methodological rigour converts a reading into a measurement.

## The five operations

### 1. Pressing (Al-Ɛaṣr) — the core move

Sūrah 103 read as an instruction rather than a lament: *"By the pressing"* — extracting the
operational content out of the surface report. The governing image is a pomegranate: peel,
bitter pulp and seeds are all genuinely part of the fruit, and none of them is what you
consume. **You press it.**

The distinction this creates is the single most important thing in the method:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **As-Ṣidq** | truthfulness | the surface report — accurate, and *not* the operational layer |
| **Al-Ḥaqq** | truth | what the mechanism actually does |

**As-Ṣidq is not false.** This is the part most often mangled. The surface reading is
truthful; it is simply not the layer where the function lives. Treating a surface reading
as a *lie* is a misuse of the method — the claim is about layers, not about honesty.

### 2. Divarication (tafseel)

Take a root and lay out its branch meanings as a **family generated by one invariant**,
rather than as a list of unrelated homonyms. The discipline: a proposed reading must sit
inside the root's attested semantic field. Anything that requires the field to stretch is a
**generated hypothesis**, not a derivation.

### 3. Nested interpretation

A term inside a passage may itself be a compressed passage. Readings are resolved
**innermost-first**, and an outer reading may not contradict an inner one already fixed.
This is what makes the method auditable: the nesting order is a dependency graph, and a
reading that violates it is out of order rather than merely disagreed with.

### 4. Lexicon discipline

103:3 — *"toiled in accordance with the proper lexicon."* Every load-bearing term must have
one stipulated operational sense, **fixed before it is used in an argument** and held
constant across the whole derivation. Silent sense-shifting mid-argument is the failure
mode this rules out, and it is the textual analogue of the pre-registration rule.

### 5. Derivation order (the prerequisite chain)

The source material states its dependencies explicitly — *"the series on Sulaymān is a
PREREQUISITE"*, *"watch YT93 before you can appreciate what we conclude here."*

**Conclusions are not standalone.** Every OQM reading carries an ordered chain of prior
derivations, and quoting a conclusion without its chain is quoting an unsupported claim.
When you present an OQM result, present the chain — or say that you are stating it without
one.

## Two conclusions from the source that constrain the method

Recorded because they are load-bearing for the governance readings elsewhere in this
repository, and because they came from the primary material rather than from us:

- **Barakah is earned, and it is an increase in knowledge that benefits others.** Two
  stated conditions: **sincerity in seeking** and **selflessness**. Structurally that is a
  two-hop channel — an inward acquisition condition and an outward distribution condition —
  which is why `E = U · D_enc · D_dec` is a *product*: either hop at zero collapses the
  result. `[L2]`
- **Selection is not conferred at birth.** The source argues explicitly against
  status-at-birth, on the grounds that it would make selection arbitrary. This is why
  selection in the governance register is `D_enc · D_dec` — *earned* — and not a membership
  identity. `[L2]`

## Auditing a claimed OQM derivation

Run this before accepting any reading, including your own:

- [ ] Is the **lexicon fixed** before the argument, and held constant throughout?
- [ ] Is the reading inside the root's **attested field**, or does it require stretching?
- [ ] Is the **derivation order** stated, or is a conclusion being quoted bare?
- [ ] Is **As-Ṣidq being called false** rather than non-operational? (misuse)
- [ ] Is a **contested decomposition** asserted as settled? (see `geometric-root-translation`)
- [ ] Is the conclusion tagged `[L2]`, or is it borrowing empirical credibility?

## The hard boundary

The method **cannot** produce:

- a measurement of any real system,
- support for a physical, economic or biological claim,
- evidence that a system built on its vocabulary will work.

It **can** produce a coherent operational vocabulary, a design generator, and a reading
that is auditable rather than merely asserted. That is a real contribution and it is worth
having — provided it is never cashed in as evidence.

**Worked example of the boundary holding.** The method generated the two-register split in
the settlement design. The measurement then found the routing rule on top of it was
**beaten by 18 of 20 random assignments**, and that the largest effect in the whole system
was a component the method never named. The method got the vocabulary right and the
performance claim wrong — which is exactly the division of labour it should expect.
