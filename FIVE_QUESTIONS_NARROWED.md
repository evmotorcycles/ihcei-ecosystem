## Three corrections to the working summary

These matter more than the wins, because an unchallenged overstatement is what gets quoted.

1. **`d² = κ·τ_rt` is not established.** It is a pre-registered proposal with four named
   laboratory experiments — OTOC butterfly-front scaling, the κ-transfer test, a tunable-range
   boundary, and a mandatory hardware negative control. **None has been run.** Describe it as
   awaiting test, never as a finding.

2. **"Two-hop is mandatory on interbank networks" overstates the record.** On the real 2016
   network the forms came out effectively **tied** — 0.6090 for the product form against
   0.6109 for the symmetric rival, which was marginally *ahead*. The later SDL run did label
   interbank a two-hop win, but **that run's own self-audit declared it uninformative**
   (Δ = 0.1536 against a floor of 0.20), so it cannot be cited in either direction.

3. **Q4 was not "vindicated."** The pre-registration discipline held — that is a fact about
   our method, not about reference-locking in the world. Treating methodological hygiene as
   empirical support would be precisely the error the hygiene exists to prevent.

## The Layer 2 readings — vocabulary, not measurement

**All of the following are `[L2]`.** They are operational definitions: defensible, auditable,
and *not findings*. Root analysis appears in none of the computations, and a test scans the
source of each runner to enforce that.

| Term | Read as a label | Read as a function `[L2]` |
|---|---|---|
| **Free will** | unlimited autonomy, or none | **Bounded capacity.** Not infinite options — consequential choice inside an assigned budget, with symmetric accounting: what is earned credited, what is deliberately incurred debited, never beyond the budget issued |
| **Takleef** | an arbitrary religious burden | **The issued load.** The point at which a capacity budget is assigned and accountability switches on. The root family is effort and friction, not punishment |
| **Adam** | one man at one moment | **A stand-in variable** — any node selected to carry the standard into its setting. A reading of the text; **not a historical claim and not a measurement** |
| **Khilafah** | a centralised empire | **A network with no centre.** Every node connected directly to the standard; none in the middle to hoard the routing |
| **Salat** | a physical posture | **The receiving hop** `D_enc` — sifting what is true from what looks true |
| **Zakat** | a fixed percentage | **The giving hop** `D_dec` — passing on the *method*, which is what prevents any one place hoarding the ability to judge |

### The boundary, and the case that proves it is real

Reading a word as a function **generates a hypothesis and a vocabulary. It never generates
evidence.**

The demonstration is in this repository: the method produced the **two-register structure**
for the settlement design — and the measurement then found the routing rule built on it was
**beaten by 18 of 20 random assignments**, with the largest effect in the whole system coming
from a component the method never named. Right vocabulary, wrong performance claim. That is
the division of labour the method should expect, and it is why the boundary is worth keeping.

### One consistency note on the multiplicative reading

The `E → 0` collapse when either hop drops to zero is the **defining prediction** of the
product form — and it now carries a stated domain limit from `6cb42dcd`: **it does not hold
where records are redundantly copied across the decoding substrate.** Any governance reading
that leans on "hoarding collapses the yield" is leaning on a claim that is **scoped**, not
universal, and should be stated with the scope attached.

## Why the nulls are what make this defensible

The standard charge against any interpretive framework is that it can never be wrong. A
framework that explains every outcome explains nothing, and nobody outside it has reason to
engage.

This body of work is the answer, and the answer is made almost entirely of failures:

- a **195×** error published against the programme's own central equation
- a selector rule recorded at **1 of 4**
- a prediction whose direction was right and **location wrong**, said so
- a sentence **withdrawn from a pre-registration** when it turned out to over-claim
- a self-audit that **voided a result** the programme would have liked

Each is a place the framework could have been protected and was not. What OQM gains is not a
bigger claim but **a smaller one that can be checked** — it keeps what it is genuinely good at
(operational vocabulary, auditable readings) and stops borrowing credibility from numbers it
did not earn. The empty shelves are labelled, so anyone can try to fill one.

**Nothing here settles a question of belief.** These are measurements of networks, ledgers and
equations. Two instruments can agree perfectly and still differ in what they mean.
