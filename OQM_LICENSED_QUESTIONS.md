## Why the refutation is worth more than the passes

Q5 tested whether watching a process beats reading a structure. τ_v scored **0.5947**
against static capacity's **0.8000**, and combined (0.7998) shows it adds nothing. The
failure survives removing the cohort's outcome-built stratum (0.5850 vs 0.6801). `[L1]`

τ_v has now failed as an **admission screen** five times, and passed once as a **covenant
trigger** (+42,840 over hold-everything). One word was doing two jobs — a Folder Icon Error:
identical surface, opposite directive. `[L2]`

## Why Q4 extends rather than confirms

*Shirk* as **reference-multiplication** was a definition. The execution kernel makes it an
instrument: single reference, structurally unable to read the claim, **AUC 1.0000** against
ground truth where a two-reference text evaluator sat at **0.5365** — chance. The DCM floor
that voided five consecutive runs was cleared at **0.3900**, unchanged. `[L1]`

The gain is the **boundary**: the same idea applied to prose produced an evaluator
correlating −0.4831 with *word count*. A reference-lock needs ground truth, and OQM did not
previously say so.

- **Measurement alarm on `D`.** ρ(capacity, D) = **+0.5695** on real PyPI, **−0.4702** on the

  rests on an unstable construct until that resolves. `[L1]`
- **The manuscript's own §3.3c domain limit was refuted** this round: where the decode hop
  has no two-step substitute, *both* forms went anti-predictive (0.4455, 0.4383). `[L1]`
- The rendered-interface reading remains an **interpretive prior**. Nothing measured here
  confirms or refutes it, and no dataset in this repository adjudicates it. `[L3]`
- **A null is not evidence for a reading.** These results constrain what may be *claimed*;
  they do not confirm what is *read*. `[L2]`
