Paste what the assistant gave you

Three things come back, and they are kept apart because they are three different kinds of answer: what in the text could be checked, what a workable plan still has to settle, and whether this is a subject where being wrong hurts.

Nothing leaves this device. What you paste is not sent anywhere, is not stored, and there is nothing here that could send it.

Before anything else

Check this one first

What a workable plan still has to settle Counted, not measured. These are words being matched, so treat a tick as a hint and a gap as a question worth asking.

What this is not telling you

  • This does not understand what you pasted.
  • This does not certify that anything is safe.
  • A claim that is completely made up reads exactly like a true one here.

Then go and check

  • Open whatever it named first and confirm it exists and says this.
  • For anything that could hurt you or cost you, ask somebody qualified before you act.

The three readings are never added together. What can be checked is measured. What a plan has to settle is counted. Whether the subject is risky is a word match. There is no single number that combines them honestly, and a tool that showed one would be doing the thing this page exists to catch.
