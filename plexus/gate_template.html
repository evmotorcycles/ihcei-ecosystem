Why there is no confidence score here

The obvious design asks each step how faithfully it carried the meaning, multiplies those numbers together, and stops the assistant when the product drops below a line. That gate was built and then retired in this project, on its own data: the sensor that would supply the number reads nothing at all on about three quarters of records, and a pre-registered test on a fresh, unseen set of nearly five thousand cases came back with a flat null.

So what is measured instead is something every organisation already records without any new instrument: how long problems you have already flagged stay open. Rising time-to-fix separated the projects that survived from the ones that died — twenty days against fifty. It is compared only against your own past, never against somebody else's number.

The three readings are never added together. A boundary, a spanning-tree measurement and a latency trend are different kinds of quantity, and there is no arithmetic that combines them honestly. A single “assistant safety score” would be the most saleable thing this page could show and the least true.
