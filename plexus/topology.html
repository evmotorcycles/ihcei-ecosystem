What the ring is Integrity is (parts − pieces) / (parts − 1). The top of that fraction is the total every link carries, which Foster's theorem fixes exactly; the bottom is the most it could ever be. No pass mark was chosen. 100% means in one piece, not correct. Why the picture tightens Distance here is √(bearing / strength), which is the same metric the map uses. Raising a strength cannot raise any distance, so turning a coupling up must pull its ends together. The space closing is the arithmetic, not an animation. Try the two buttons Weakest and load-bearing are different questions, and sometimes one link answers both. On An energy bill the weakest link is also the only route to the late fee, so removing it drops the ring to 83%. On Getting to work the weakest link is one of two ways to the station, so removing it costs nothing and the ring stays at 100%. The line under the picture reports integrity measured before and after, so it tells you which of those actually happened instead of a sentence fixed in advance.
