List the parts, say which one is the result, then join up whatever is worked out from whatever. Nothing you type leaves this device.

Nothing here was scored There is nothing to tune and no weighting to argue with. On the second tab the percentages add up to the number of parts minus the number of separate pieces, always, as a matter of arithmetic — so no step can be made to look more important without another looking less. Turning everything up does nothing Multiply the strength of every link by any number you like and not one percentage moves. Two different questions Holding it up asks whether there is another way round a step. Resting on asks whether there is another way in. They can disagree, and when they do it is worth reading both. What it cannot tell you Whether a step is useful, or whether a source is true. A required form that changes no outcome still reads 100%, because removing it does break the chain. And it only knows the parts you entered. Nothing leaves this device No account, no network, no server. It works with the aeroplane mode on.
