How it is worded

Two measurements, never one What a claim rests on and how it is worded are different questions and they disagree often. Writing that hands judgement back can still rest on a single unverified post; blunt, pushy writing can be perfectly sound. There is no combined score here and there will not be one. What the wording check is A list of phrases, counted. It does not read grammar and does not understand the text: "you can verify" inside "you can verify nothing" counts the wrong way. A careful liar scores clean. Treat it as a prompt to look, never as a verdict. What it cannot tell you Whether anything is true. It only knows the lines you marked and the joins you drew. Two sources that secretly share an origin you did not enter will read as independent, and it will be wrong. Nothing leaves this device No account, no network, no server. Flint reads what you paste, and that is the only way anything gets in.
