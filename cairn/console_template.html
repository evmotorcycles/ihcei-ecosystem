What this app is for. Paste a claim. Cairn reports how much checkable evidence is in the wording — and declines when the text does not determine an answer. It routes definitions, opinions and questions to out of scope with no score at all.

Live engine

The interactive chat lives in cairn.html, backed by ei_llm.py. Run python3 cairn/ei_server.py to wire the Python engine to the browser.
