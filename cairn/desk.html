Pick a tool. Nothing here leaves your device — no account, no internet, nothing uploaded. Every answer also tells you what it could not work out.

Before you rely on any of these None of them knows whether something is true. They check whether a claim can be checked, whether permission was granted, and whether a record was altered. A sentence can pass every check here and still be wrong. Use them to find your questions — then, when it matters, take those questions to a person.

The Valet KeyHand over just enough. A valet key starts the car — it does not open the boot.

This is what you are giving an app or an assistant. Anything not on this list is refused automatically, so you never have to guess what it might ask for.

What a valet key cannot do It stops an app reaching what you never handed over. It does not stop it misusing what you did hand over, and it cannot tell you whether the app is trustworthy. Give the smallest key that does the job.
The DashcamA running record. If anyone edits an old entry, the seal breaks and you see it.
What a dashcam cannot do It makes tampering visible, not impossible. And it says nothing about whether an entry was true when it was written — somebody can still record a lie; they just cannot rewrite it later without it showing.
The Confidence MeterWhen a tool says it is sure, is it right that often? We checked ourselves.
We failed our own test We tested whether our claim-checker's confidence matches an independent measure it never sees. We set the limit at 0.15 before looking. We measured 0.3727. We did not move the limit.

Grey is what it said about itself. Green is what was independently true. Every bar leans the same way — it was consistently less sure than it should have been.

The File OpenerOpen a document, spreadsheet, transcript, photo or code — and see what is really in it.
What the opener cannot do It reads shape, never meaning. There is no eyesight here — a photo of a chart, a photo of a contract and a photo of a cat are the same thing to it. And there is no internet, so it cannot fetch a video or a web page: open the video, copy the transcript, paste it in.
