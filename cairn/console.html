What this app is for. Paste a claim. Cairn reports how much checkable evidence is in the wording — and declines when the text does not determine an answer. It routes definitions, opinions and questions to out of scope with no score at all.

Live engine

The interactive chat lives in cairn.html, backed by ei_llm.py. Run python3 cairn/ei_server.py to wire the Python engine to the browser.

Audits

Empirical claims — statements about what is measurably the case.

Declines

Definitions, opinions, questions. Neutral, no score, not a failure.

What CI is for. Every other app audits something else. CI audits the auditor: is the confidence Cairn reports actually trustworthy, does an interaction leave you with more options, and can you check the result without the system?

Diagnosis, stated rather than used as a rescue. Engine confidence measures how checkable the SENTENCE is; ground truth measures how sound the PROJECT is. Different quantities, diverging by 0.37 on real data.
What this means for you. Cairn confidence is NOT a probability the claim is true and NOT a quality score for the thing described. It measures the text.
