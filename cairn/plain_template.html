Governance Console

Six small tools for checking things you were told.

No account, no internet connection, nothing sent anywhere. Everything below runs inside this page on your own device. Pick the thing you actually need.

What these tools cannot do None of them knows whether something is true. They check whether a claim is checkable, whether permission was actually granted, and whether a record has been altered. A sentence can pass every check here and still be wrong. Use them to see what you're missing — not as a verdict on reality.

Check a claim

Paste a statement someone gave you — from a person, an article, or an AI. You'll get back what can be checked in it, what's missing, and what to ask for next.

What this cannot do A permission list stops an app reaching things it was never allowed to reach. It does not stop an app misusing what you did allow, and it cannot tell whether the app is trustworthy. Grant the smallest thing that gets the job done.

    Open a file and see what is really in it

    Choose a file, or paste text below. Nothing is uploaded — the file is read inside this page on your own device.

    What these readers cannot do They parse structure, never meaning. A spreadsheet reader knows a column is numeric; it does not know what the number means. The image reader knows the pixel size and nothing else — there is no vision model here, so a chart, a contract photo and a cat are the same thing to it. There is no network, so no video or web page can be fetched: open it, copy the transcript, paste it in. Every result lists its own blind spots, and those lists are the important part.

    Plan something so you can tell if it worked

    Most plans list what you will do. This one makes you write down what would count as failure — before you start, while you can still be honest about it.

    What the planner cannot do It gives you a structure, not advice. It does not know your field, cannot tell you whether the goal is achievable or wise, and cannot estimate cost or time. For anything touching health, money, law or safety it will say so — because a well-structured plan and a safe plan are different things.
