Before you rely on any of this None of it knows whether something is true. It checks whether a claim can be checked, whether permission was given, and whether a record was altered. What this gate cannot do It stops what comes through it. It cannot stop a program that does not come through it at all — a program that opens its own connection is unaffected. Making it unavoidable is a job for the machine underneath: a container, or a network with no other way out. That part is real and standard, and it is not built here. A gate you can walk around is a gate you should not rely on alone.
